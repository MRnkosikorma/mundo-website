Ask us on WhatsApp
How do I use Mundo?

Start a WhatsApp conversation with Mundo and follow the prompts to pay a fare or use another available service.

Do I need to download an app?

No separate Mundo app is required. The service is accessed through WhatsApp on your phone.

How do I pay a fare?

Add funds to your Mundo balance using the options shown in the chat. To pay a fare, select the option, enter a participating vehicle’s registration plate, review the fare, and confirm.

What if I need help with a payment?

Keep your WhatsApp confirmation and message the Mundo team using the official number on this page. Never share your EcoCash PIN, banking password, or one-time security code in chat.

Where can I use Mundo?

Mundo is live in Bulawayo, starting with the NUST–CBD commuter corridor and participating transport operators. Check the Mundo chat for current availability.
