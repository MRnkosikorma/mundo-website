1. Wallet Top-Ups

Commuters top up their Mundo wallet balance through supported Zimbabwean payment channels (including EcoCash). Funds added to your balance remain available for transit fares on participating vehicles.

2. Fare Deductions & Receipts

When you enter a vehicle plate number and confirm the fare prompt in WhatsApp, the exact trip fare is debited from your Mundo balance. You instantly receive a timestamped digital receipt with a unique transaction code.

3. Dispute & Refund Window

If you accidentally enter an incorrect vehicle registration plate or experience a duplicate deduction:

  • Report the transaction in chat by replying with HELP or messaging the support line within 2 hours of the journey.
  • Retain your WhatsApp confirmation receipt containing the transaction ID.
  • Investigated and validated duplicate charges will be re-credited to your Mundo wallet balance within 24 hours.

4. Fraud Prevention

Transactions deemed fraudulent, abusive, or violating Zimbabwe transit regulations are subject to immediate suspension and regulatory review.
