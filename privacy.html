Data Minimisation Guarantee: Under Section 11 of the Zimbabwe Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07], we collect only the minimal data strictly required to deliver digital transit fare payments.

1. Data Controller Identification

For the purposes of the Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07], the Data Controller for personal data processed through Mundo is:

  • Entity: Mundo Mobility Initiative
  • Jurisdiction: Bulawayo, Republic of Zimbabwe
  • Regulatory Oversight: Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ)
  • Contact Channel: Official WhatsApp Support at +263 78 870 3767

2. What We Collect and Why

  • Phone Number (MSISDN): Used solely to identify your transit session and send WhatsApp ride receipts.
  • EcoCash / Mobile Money Account: Used strictly to credit your transit balance and settle driver disbursements.
  • Vehicle Registration Plates: Recorded during fare check-in to credit the transport operator.

3. What We Never Collect

Zero-Chat-Trail Security: We NEVER ask for, store, or log your EcoCash PIN, banking passwords, or OTP security codes.

No GPS Geolocation Tracking: We do NOT access your live GPS coordinates or physical location history. Rides are processed solely through vehicle plate lookups.

4. Your Data Protection Rights

Under Chapter 12:07, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of your transaction history.
  • Request deletion of your account and disassociation of your phone number.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by messaging STOP in WhatsApp.
